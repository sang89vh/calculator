Frequently asked

What is in the app?
Three calculators and several tools. Basic is a plain calculator with percent and memory. Market Scale is a weight × price calculator for market and shop sellers, with a cart, change due and a day log. Scientific is one full scientific calculator with eleven modes behind a searchable mode sheet: expressions, complex numbers, statistics, base-n, equations, matrices, tables, vectors, inequalities, ratios and distributions. The Worksheet works down the page line by line and shows how each answer was reached; a graph plots y = f(x), equations, polar and parametric curves; a trigonometry reference, a periodic table of all 118 elements, the unit converter and the formula library are there for reference. On iPhone, iPad and Mac with Apple Intelligence, a tutor explains one problem at a time.

Which parts are free?
Basic, Market Scale, the Worksheet, the graph, the trigonometry reference, the periodic table, the unit converter and the formulas are free forever. Scientific is free for a year from the day you first open the app, with a countdown in the app; after that it needs Calculators ProX Premium: a lifetime unlock, a yearly plan or a monthly plan. The yearly and monthly plans renew automatically until cancelled. The lifetime unlock is a single purchase and never expires. Everything you already saved stays yours either way.

I paid before, but a new phone is asking me to pay again.
Open Settings inside the app, tap the unlock entry and then Restore, signed in to the same app store account you bought with. The store confirms the purchase and the unlock returns. You are never charged twice for a lifetime purchase.

Where is my data kept, and can you see it?
On your device, and no. The app has no server and no networking code, so nothing you enter can reach us even in principle. It leaves your phone only when you share it yourself, or when you turn on Apple Private Cloud Compute for the tutor on iPhone or iPad, which sends the problem and your tutor messages to Apple — never to us. See the privacy policy.

Does it work without internet?
Every calculator feature works in airplane mode. Purchasing, restoring purchases and refreshing a subscription need a connection to your app store.

Why is a result different from the calculator on my desk?
Scientific follows the conventions of handheld scientific calculators: input logic, operator precedence, displayed precision and error messages. It is an independent implementation, not a copy of any device's firmware, so a difference is possible. If you find one, send us the exact keystrokes and what your device shows — that is the most useful bug report we can get.

How do I price by lạng or per 100 g?
In Market Scale, tap the price basis next to the unit price and choose per kg, per lạng or per 100 g. The amount recalculates as you type. Rounding to the nearest 100, 500 or 1,000 đ is a separate setting, and the unrounded amount stays visible underneath.

Can I move my data to a new phone?
Yes. Open Settings, then Offline and privacy, then Back up my data. You choose a passphrase, and the app writes one encrypted file you can send to yourself however you like. On the new phone, Restore from a backup, the same passphrase, and the app shows you what is in the file before it changes anything. There is still no sync and no cloud account, because both would mean your sales leaving your phone without you deciding. Your purchase restores separately.

What happens if I forget the backup passphrase?
The file cannot be opened, by us or by anyone. The passphrase is not stored anywhere and there is no reset, because a reset would be a way in for someone who is not you. Keep it somewhere you would keep a key.

Can I stop other people opening my sales?
Yes. Settings, then Lock sales and shop details. After that the sales log, the saved goods and the shop details ask for your face, fingerprint or passcode before they open. The calculators and the till are never locked, so you can hand the phone across the counter without unlocking anything.

Is my data encrypted on the phone?
Your sales, saved goods and shop details are, with a key the phone itself holds and never shares. The calculators' own settings and history are ordinary app storage. Settings, then Offline and privacy tells you which of the two your phone managed.

How do I delete everything?
Settings, then Offline and privacy, then Delete all data. It asks twice, and then it is gone from the phone. Nothing is held anywhere else, so there is nobody to write to. Your purchase and your trial are not affected.

What is exam mode?
One switch in Settings, under Exam mode, that hides the camera scan, the steps, the tutor, practice problems, voice input on Mac and Siri requests. The calculators, the Worksheet's answers, graphs and the formula library keep working. Turning it on or off asks you to confirm, and while it is on a badge at the top of the screen shows the time it went on. It is a setting on this device only and is never put in a backup. It does not stop anyone leaving the app, so follow your exam's own rules.

No. This is an independent product. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Casio Computer Co., Ltd.
