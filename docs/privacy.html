Scientific Calculators ProX — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-16

Scientific Calculators ProX works fully offline. Nothing you type into it ever leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app collects no personal data of any kind, and there is no account to create.

What we do not collect

We never collect: your name, email address, phone number or any account identifier — the app has no sign-in; usage analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers or any other telemetry; your location, contacts, photos, calendar, microphone, camera or health data; and none of what you calculate — expressions, results, history, market-scale presets, carts and day logs are written to your device and never transmitted.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings, the calculator mode you last used, and each mode's own state.
  • Calculation history for the scientific modes.
  • Market Scale data: saved goods, tares, the current cart, the day log, and the seller name and phone number if you choose to enter them for receipt footers.
  • The date you first opened the app and whether you have bought a plan or the lifetime unlock. These live in the system keychain (iOS) or the app's private keystore area (Android) so that reinstalling does not restart your free month or make you pay twice. Neither identifies you.

Everything else lives in the app's private storage area, which the operating system keeps unreadable to other apps.

Network access

The app contains no developer-operated networking code. There is no analytics library, crash reporter or advertising SDK. On Android, the Google Play billing library adds the store's billing entitlement plus network permissions that it uses only to communicate with Google Play about purchases. The app sends no data to the developer. An automated test in our build fails if anyone adds an unreviewed dependency that would change this boundary.

Purchases

Classic and Market Scale are free forever. Advanced and Ultimate are free for 30 days from your first launch. After that, you can choose a monthly plan, a yearly plan, or a one-time lifetime unlock. Plans renew automatically until cancelled; the lifetime unlock never expires.

  • Apple App Store — processes purchases on iOS and tells the app whether an entitlement is active.
  • Google Play — processes purchases on Android and tells the app whether an entitlement is active.

We never see payment details and operate no purchase-verification server. Restoring a purchase asks the relevant store the same question. No purchase data reaches the developer at any point.

When you share something

Copying a result, sharing a receipt or exporting a day log as CSV hands that text to the app you pick — a messaging app, an email client, the clipboard. That happens only when you tap the button, it goes wherever you send it, and it never passes through us.

How long we keep it

Your data stays on your device for as long as the app is installed. Deleting the app deletes it. Because nothing is ever uploaded, there is no copy anywhere else for us to keep or delete.

Deleting your data

Clear history or delete items inside the app, or remove the app from your device to erase everything at once. There is no account to close and nothing to request from us.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date above.
