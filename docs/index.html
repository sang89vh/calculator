ElizaAI

A market scale, a step-by-step worksheet and a scientific calculator, in one offline app

ElizaAI is an offline toolbox for market sellers and students: Market Scale, a weight × price calculator built for market and shop sellers in Vietnam; the Worksheet, which works line by line and shows every step; one full scientific calculator with eleven modes; and a basic calculator, a unit converter and a formula library. Everything runs offline and nothing is collected; the only exception is Apple Private Cloud Compute for the tutor on iPhone or iPad, which you turn on yourself and which sends the problem to Apple, never to us.
