Scientific Calculators ProX

Three scientific calculators and a market-scale calculator, in one offline app

Scientific Calculators ProX puts four calculators behind one app: Classic, Advanced and Ultimate — three scientific modes, each with its own key arrangement and menu style — and Market Scale, a weight × price calculator built for market and shop sellers in Vietnam. Everything runs offline and nothing is collected.
